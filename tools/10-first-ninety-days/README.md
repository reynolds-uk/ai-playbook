# The first ninety days

Almost all of it is publishing rather than building.

## In plain terms

What makes the first three months credible is that other people can read what you did: a baseline, a register, the criteria, a scorecard, and a list of what you stopped. A kill list published beside the wins is the reason anyone trusts the wins.

## What to ask for

The six documents below, by the end of month three.

## Files

- [`plan-small.md`](plan-small.md): for a team or a company under about fifty people.
- [`plan-large.md`](plan-large.md): for a large business or group.
