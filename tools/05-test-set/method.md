# The test set · method

1. **Pick the cases.** Ask the people who do the work best to choose real cases from the last two years: clear passes, clear fails, and the awkward ones in between. Roughly two fifths passes, a third fails, a quarter awkward is a good start.
2. **Mark them.** For each case, the right outcome, and for the fails, why. Where two qualified people disagree, keep the case and record both views. The disagreement is useful.
3. **Strip what must not leave.** Remove anything that can't leave the business, but keep enough that the case is still real.
4. **Version it.** Every change to the set is dated and attributed.
5. **Keep it away from vendors.** They may run it. They never see it in advance and never write it.
6. **Run it on every change.** New model, new prompt, new data source, new version: the agent runs against the set before it keeps its grade (tool 6). The score goes on the register (tool 7).
7. **Refresh it.** Replace a share of the cases every quarter. A set nobody has touched in a year is measuring last year's business.

## Where to keep it

Somewhere your business controls, with access limited to the people who mark cases and the person who owns the register.
