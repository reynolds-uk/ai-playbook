# The model, step by step

Per step, never per workflow. Re-decide when prices move.

## In plain terms

Not every step deserves the most expensive model. Hard, rare, costly-if-wrong steps get the best you can rent. High-volume, checkable steps get the cheapest model that passes your test set, sometimes one that runs on your own machines and sends nothing anywhere. Where data rules apply, they come first, whatever the cost.

## What to ask for

A routing sheet for each workflow, the cost per outcome reconciled with finance, and how much customer material left your control per case. That last number can be checked. A policy statement can't.

The reasoning is in [Earned autonomy](https://ai.valuecreator.io/read/control#model-per-step).
