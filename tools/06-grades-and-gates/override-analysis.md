## What it tells you

- **The overrides cluster** (the same kind of case, the same reason): the agent is wrong in a specific, fixable way. Fix it before promoting it.
- **The overrides are scattered:** your own people disagree with each other. That's often the more valuable finding. Get the people together and agree what good looks like, then update the test set.
