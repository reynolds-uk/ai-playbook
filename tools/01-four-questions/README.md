# The four questions

One workflow at a time. An hour with the people who do it.

## In plain terms

Point AI at the wrong part of the job and you get a small number. Before anything is built, ask four questions about the work. The fourth is the one people skip, and it usually moves the target: most of the time a piece of work takes is waiting before and after it, not the work itself.

## What to ask for

A one-page canvas for the workflow you're about to spend money on, filled in with the people who do it. If the "end to end" time is much bigger than the "work" time, the prize is in the waiting.

## The example

An illustration: a customer's contract renewal in a software business. The work (pull the account, price it, draft it) takes about four days. The whole thing takes about seventy: waiting on usage figures before, and going round again on the same three clauses after. A drafting agent saves days. Removing the waiting saves weeks.

The reasoning is in [Where the time actually goes](https://ai.valuecreator.io/read/where-the-time-goes).
