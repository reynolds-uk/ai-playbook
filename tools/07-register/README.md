# The register

**Small team:** a shared spreadsheet, one row per agent, live in a week. **Large business:** live by the end of month two, visible to the head of every business unit.

## In plain terms

One page for every piece of software that acts in your business's name: what it does, what it may touch, how much it's allowed to do on its own, and the one named person on the hook for it. With it, you can say what is acting for you, on whose authority, how well it works and what it costs. If it isn't on the register, it doesn't run.

## What to ask for

The register. Check that every supervisor field has a person's name, not a team. Then look at it laid out as agents down the side and teams or markets across the top, with the grade in each cell. That's the picture a board should see.

The reasoning is in [Earned autonomy](https://ai.valuecreator.io/read/control#register).
