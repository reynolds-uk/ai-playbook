# The curriculum exercise

**Small team:** before your next three hires, an hour. **Large business:** an afternoon per department, before this year's intake decision.

## In plain terms

The junior work AI takes first is often how people learned the job. Sort it by two things: what the output is worth, and what doing it taught. Automate what taught nothing. Where the work was how people learned, keep a person deciding, or keep a share of it for people and call it a training budget. Otherwise the saving arrives this year and the missing experienced people arrive in five.

## What to ask for

The grid for one team, and a written decision about which work you're keeping for people because it teaches.

The reasoning is in [The bottom was the training programme](https://ai.valuecreator.io/read/people#grid).
