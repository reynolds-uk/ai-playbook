# The two columns

**Small team:** kept by whoever holds the budget, looked at monthly. **Large business:** kept by finance, reviewed quarterly, reported to the board with both columns side by side.

## In plain terms

Time saved isn't money until someone banks it. Keep two columns. In the first, everything AI is said to save. In the second, only what reached the accounts in the same quarter: the freed time went to named work that earns, or the cost actually left. A buyer's diligence team discounts the first column heavily and pays for the second.

## What to ask for

Both columns, together, with a name against every banked line. If you're given a percentage, you've been given the first column. Put the banked column in pounds wherever you can.

The reasoning is in [If the AI becomes the business, what is the business?](https://ai.valuecreator.io/read/value#claimed-vs-banked)
