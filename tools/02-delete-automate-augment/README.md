# Delete, automate, augment

Straight after the four questions, step by step, with the same people in the room.

## In plain terms

Automating a step that should have been deleted is the most expensive way to keep it. It now has a vendor, a maintenance cost and a percentage on a slide. So every step goes in one of three piles, and you delete first.

## What to ask for

The sorted list, with the deleted steps reported before anything automated. If nothing was deleted, nobody asked the question properly.

The reasoning is in [If the AI becomes the business, what is the business?](https://ai.valuecreator.io/read/value#delete-first)
