# Who owns it

**Small team:** part of someone's job. **Large business:** a role with a seat where investment is decided.

## In plain terms

The title matters less than what the role owns. It owns four documents: the register, the test set, the gate criteria and the record of decisions. It can demote an agent the business would rather keep running. And it's measured on one number: the banked column. Without the number it governs but delivers little. Without the documents it delivers numbers nobody can check.
