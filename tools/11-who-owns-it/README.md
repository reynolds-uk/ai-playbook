# Who owns it

**Small team:** a hat, not a job. **Large business:** a role with a seat where investment is decided.

## In plain terms

The title matters less than what the role owns. It owns four documents: the register, the test set, the gate criteria and the record of decisions. It can demote an agent the business would rather keep running. And it's measured on one number: the banked column. A role with the documents and no number will govern beautifully and deliver nothing. A role with the number and no documents will deliver a percentage and a liability.
