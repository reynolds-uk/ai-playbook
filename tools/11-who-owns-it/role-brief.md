# Role brief · the person who owns AI in the business

## Owns

- The register (tool 7)
- The test sets (tool 5)
- The gate criteria (tool 6)
- The record of decisions

## Can

- Demote any agent, including one a team would rather keep running
- Stop an initiative at a gate (tool 3)

## Is measured on

The banked column (tool 4), agreed with whoever runs the money.

## Sits

Where investment decisions are made.

## Small team

Usually the founder or the CTO, for a few hours a week. That's enough, as long as it's written down whose it is.

## Large business

A named role with a goal of making itself unnecessary within three years, because by then this should be how the business runs.
