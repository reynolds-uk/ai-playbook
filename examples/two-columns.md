# The two columns: Q3 2026 (July to September)

*A worked example for a fictional business, written by the plugin in a test run with a simulated user. The people and figures are invented for the test.*

Columns owned by: Dana Price, Finance Director.

## Both columns, side by side

| Initiative | Claimed | Banked in Q3 |
|---|---|---|
| Sales email drafting tool | 4 hours a person a week across 20 reps (80 hours a week) | £0 |
| Supplier-invoice matching tool | 1.5 people's worth of effort | £9,500 |
| Chatbot | Handles 40% of delivery queries | £0 |
| **Total** | **80 hours a week, 1.5 people, 40% of delivery queries. No £ figure claimed.** | **£9,500** |

The claims are in hours, people and percentages, so they have no single total. Every claim comes from the vendor. None comes from our own measurement against a baseline.

The two columns are never added together.

## Banked lines

| Initiative | Banked | Named cost line | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Supplier-invoice matching tool | £9,500 | Finance operations role (salary £38,000 a year) not refilled for all of July to September 2026. Salary only, no on-costs. | James Whitfield, Financial Controller |

## Claims not yet banked, and what it would take

1. **Sales email drafting tool: 80 hours a week claimed, £0 banked.** Owner: Priya Shah, Head of Sales. Reps are doing the same work as before. To bank it, name the work the freed hours go to, such as a named extra target or territory, and show the result in Q4. The alternative is a cost that leaves, such as contracted hours. First, measure the time against a baseline of our own rather than relying on the vendor's pilot survey.
2. **Chatbot: 40% of delivery queries claimed, £0 banked.** Owner: unknown, find out. Contact centre headcount and rotas are unchanged. To bank it, name an owner, then check the 40% against our own ticket volumes. After that, either cut hours in the rota, leave a vacancy unfilled, or move agents onto named work with a result.
3. **Supplier-invoice matching tool: the remaining 0.5 person, £0 banked.** Owner: James Whitfield, Financial Controller. One role left. The other half of the claim has no line yet. To bank it, name the work the remaining freed effort goes to, or a cost that leaves, such as overtime or contractor hours.

## How the columns are kept

Dana Price keeps both columns. Update them each quarter from what reached the accounts. Any board report or plan carries both columns, never only the claimed one.
